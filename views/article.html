Central bank minutes point to a slower pace of rate cuts

Macro Brief 24 min ago 4 min read

Minutes from the latest policy meeting suggest officials are in no hurry to ease further. Several members said inflation was still running above target in services, and argued that more evidence was needed before the next move.

What it means for markets

Traders trimmed bets on an early cut, which helped the dollar and pushed short-dated yields higher. Equity index futures were little changed, while gold held its recent range as investors weighed firmer real yields against steady safe-haven demand.

The next inflation report is the main test. A softer print would revive expectations of easing, while a hot one would support the case for holding rates through the winter.
